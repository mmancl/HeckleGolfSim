What's Inside

Everything a Golfer Needs

From casual putting practice to full 18-hole rounds with friends — IT Golf has you covered, for free.

🗺️

Real-World Courses via OpenStreetMap

Download and play virtually any golf course in the world using real OpenStreetMap data. No artificial course library limitations.

🆓

Completely Free to Play

No subscriptions, no license fees, no paywalls. IT Golf is fully free — just download and tee off.

📡

Launch Monitor Support

Compatible with popular launch monitors so you can bring real swing data into your simulator sessions.

🎯

Minigames & Practice Modes

Sharpen your short game with putting and chipping challenges, closest-to-pin contests, and loft control drills.

👥

Multiplayer & Cross-Platform

Play against friends across Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS — all in the same lobby.

📊

Stat Tracking & Leaderboards

Track your round history, scoring trends, and compete on online leaderboards. Sign in to view your stats from any device.

🏌️

Full Course Play

Tee-to-green with scorecard tracking, overhead maps, and realistic hole layouts sourced from real course data.

📱

Mobile-First Design

Designed for touch on Android and iOS with an intuitive UI, while also supporting full keyboard & mouse on desktop.

⚙️

Advanced Shot Controls

Shape control, loft adjustment, and power mechanics give skilled players deep control over every shot.
