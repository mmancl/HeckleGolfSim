### New Features
- **Separated Web Portal Swing Analysis & Achievements Tabs**: Split the previously bundled "Swing Analysis & Achievements" tab on the web Player Portal into two distinct, dedicated navigation sections: **🎯 Swing Analysis** and **🏆 Achievements**:
  - **Dedicated Swing Analysis View**: Features categorized swing recommendations and flaw tendencies, presenting both **Current Month Running Totals** and **All-Time Running Totals** in a clean, responsive card grid matching the in-game display, alongside prior months' historical logs and swing history reset controls.
  - **Dedicated Achievements Showcase**: Highlights unlocked achievement badges, challenge criteria, and unlock dates in an unobstructed grid layout.
- **Web Player Portal Improvement Analytics & Trends Tab**: Added a dedicated **📈 Improvement Analytics** section to the Heckle Golf Simulator Player Portal (`portal.html`), empowering players to review their long-term performance trends and see if their game is improving:
  - **Offline Dispersion & Target Accuracy Trends**: Tracks whether your lateral offline dispersion is gravitating closer to the centerline target or expanding off-target. Displays your recent versus earlier baseline average offline yards, percentage of shots landing in the fairway/target corridor (±10 yds), and left versus right miss tendencies.
  - **Club Delivery Alignment Trends (Face Angle & Club Path)**: Analyzes whether your club face is squaring up towards 0.0° square at impact and whether your swing path is straightening towards neutral down the target line. Highlights the face-to-path differential, helping players identify whether their ball curvature (slice or hook tendency) is being eliminated.
  - **Strike Quality, Centeredness & Smash Factor Progression**: Monitors energy transfer and ball compression over time, tracking whether impact locations are converging toward the sweet spot (mm from center face) and if smash factor is trending into the optimal compression window (1.45–1.50).
  - **Interactive Visual Trend Charts**: Features three responsive, high-performance SVG visual trend charts with moving average trendlines:
    - *Directional Offline Dispersion Trendline* (highlighting shots relative to the 0 yds centerline and ±10 yds fairway corridor).
    - *Club Delivery Convergence Chart* (illustrating Face Angle and Club Path curves converging toward the 0.0° straight line).
    - *Smash Factor & Strike Consistency Chart* (showing shot-by-shot smash factor progression with optimal compression bands).
  - **Club & Sample Window Filtering**: Easily toggle between "All Recorded Clubs (Aggregated)" or drill down into individual clubs (Driver, Woods, Irons, Wedges), with options to analyze "Last 10 vs Prior 10 Shots", "Last 25 vs Prior 25", or "All Recorded Shots".
  - **Detailed Baseline vs. Recent Breakdown Table & AI Swing Coach Insights**: Provides a side-by-side comparison table of earlier baseline shots versus recent swings across all key metrics, accompanied by personalized, actionable AI swing coach tips celebrating your biggest improvements and highlighting high-impact drills.
- **Bushnell GC3 & FSX CSV Integration Tab**: Added a dedicated **⛳ Bushnell GC3 (FSX)** tab to the Launch Monitor startup connection menu. This integration allows players with a Bushnell GC3, Bushnell Launch Pro, or Foresight launch monitor to connect directly to Heckle Golf Simulator by reading live CSV shot exports from FSX Play or FSX 2020 without needing expensive third-party bridge tools or paid software integrations.
  - **Real-Time Shot Telemetry Tracking**: Monitors the exported `shots.csv` or `LastShot.csv` file in real-time. As soon as you hit a shot on your launch monitor, Heckle Golf Simulator immediately parses ball speed, vertical and horizontal launch angles, total spin, backspin, sidespin, and spin axis—along with club head speed, club path, angle of attack, face angle, and dynamic loft—to simulate authentic ball flight with full physics and commentary.
  - **Flexible Path Configuration & Auto-Detect**: Enter your CSV path manually, click the **📂 Browse...** file dialog to select the file or folder on your PC, or click **🔍 Auto-Detect** to automatically scan standard Foresight Sports and FSX Play / 2020 install and document paths.
  - **Customizable Polling Rates**: Select between Fast (100 ms), Standard (250 ms), and Battery Saver (500 ms) live file sync intervals.
  - **Interactive Test Sample Shot**: Features a "🏌️ Test Sample Shot" button directly on the tab so you can immediately test ball flight and audio feedback before taking a full swing.
  - **Auto-Connect on Startup**: Fully integrated with the "Remember device and auto-connect on startup" option so your Bushnell GC3 connection is preserved and automatically resumes monitoring every time you launch the simulator.
- **Free Camera Fly Mode & Comprehensive Elevation Controls**: Added an interactive free camera mode that allows players to freely fly anywhere across golf courses to scout terrain, greens, bunkers, hazards, and scenic vistas without needing to hit a ball.
- **Flight Controls via Analog Stick, Keyboard, Mouse, and On-Screen HUD**:
  - **Full Elevation (Up & Down) Navigation**: Smoothly ascend and descend using mouse scroll wheel (scroll up to elevate, scroll down to lower), middle-mouse button drag, Space / E / R / PageUp to fly up, Ctrl / Q / C / F / PageDown to descend, Shift + Up / Shift + Down arrow keys, or gamepad triggers and D-pad.
  - **On-Screen Free Camera HUD Overlay**: Features a sleek floating control card with quick-tap or hold `▲ Up` and `▼ Down` altitude buttons, quick shortcuts reference, and a dedicated `⛳ Ball View` button.
  - **Instant Return to Standard Ball View**: Exiting Free Camera mode (via the in-menu toggle, Escape key, gamepad B button, or on-screen `⛳ Ball View` button) now automatically repositions and re-aligns the camera back behind your ball into the standard address view facing your target.
  - **Analog Sticks**: Navigate forward, backward, and strafe using the left analog stick while smoothly panning and pitching the camera with the right stick. Adjust elevation with triggers or D-pad, and boost fly speed with the left stick button.
  - **Arrow Keys & WASD**: Fly in the direction you are looking with Up/Down arrow keys or W/S, steer and turn with Left/Right arrow keys, strafe with A/D, pitch up/down with Alt+Up/Down or Home/End, ascend with Space/E/R/PageUp, descend with Ctrl/Q/C/F/PageDown, and boost speed with Shift.
  - **Click-to-Move**: Simply click or tap anywhere on the course to display a waypoint marker and smoothly glide the camera to that spot at a comfortable viewing height. Any manual key or analog stick input instantly takes over manual control.
  - **Terrain Clearance Protection**: Automatic ground height clamping prevents the camera from clipping underground or dipping below the turf.
- **Visuals Submenu in Toggle Helpers Menu**: Added a new **👁️ Visuals Menu** under the Toggle Helpers panel (alongside the Immersion and Training menus). Inside the Visuals submenu, a dedicated **🎥 Free Camera** toggle button lets players easily activate or deactivate Free Camera mode at any time. Pressing Escape or the controller B button also instantly exits Free Camera mode and restores the normal address camera.
- **In-Game Graphics Quality Toggle in Visuals Helper Menu**: Added a dedicated **🎨 Graphics** toggle button directly inside the **👁️ Visuals Menu** under Toggle Helpers (accessible during both Course Play and Driving Range modes). Players can effortlessly tap or click to cycle between **Low (Fast Stylized)**, **Medium (Balanced PBR)**, and **High (Photorealistic)** quality on the fly without needing to pause or navigate into the full Settings menu. Right-clicking reverses the cycle, and changes automatically apply in real-time with smooth loading spinner feedback.
- **Shot Replay Camera Follow Option**: Added a "Shot Replay Follow" toggle under Camera Settings. By default, the camera remains stationed at the address view to show the ball flight into the distance when viewing other players' shots. Enabling this option activates follow camera tracking so you can dynamically follow opponent shot replays across the fairway and green from launch to rest.
- **Host Gimme Rules for Multiplayer Matches & Tournaments**: Added a new "Gimme Rules" option in multiplayer round setup when hosting online matches and tournaments. Hosts can choose to let players use their own local gimme settings or force specific gimme rules (selecting which gimme tiers +1, +2, and +3 are active and setting their radius sizes in feet) for everyone in the match or tournament.
- **Non-Destructive Forced Gimme Settings**: When joining a match or tournament with forced gimme rules, all players automatically use the host's configured gimme parameters and visual green rings during the round without modifying or overwriting their personal gimme settings on disk. Once the round or match concludes, players seamlessly return to their own local settings without needing to reconfigure anything.
- **Remember Device & Auto-Connect**: Added a "Remember device and auto-connect on startup" checkbox to the Launch Monitor connection screen. When checked, Heckle Golf Simulator remembers your launch monitor details and seamlessly auto-connects in the background upon launching the game, skipping the startup connection popup. If left unchecked, the connection screen will continue to appear on game startup so you can choose or reconfigure devices.
- **Encrypted Bluetooth Tab & Blue Tees Rainmaker Connector**: Added a dedicated **🔐 Encrypted Bluetooth** tab to the Launch Monitor Connect Modal for devices requiring explicit selection and dynamic PIN / pairing code handshakes (such as Blue Tees Rainmaker and direct BLE devices like Rapsodo MLM2PRO).
- **Explicit Connection Workflow**: Unlike auto-detect standard Bluetooth, the Encrypted Bluetooth tab requires explicitly selecting the launch monitor model and entering the on-device display PIN code before connecting.
- **Blue Tees Rainmaker Integration**: Added full C# connector infrastructure under `addons/launch_monitors/rainmaker/` (`RainmakerLaunchMonitor`, `RainmakerConnectionSession`, `RainmakerProtocol`, `RainmakerGodotMapper`, and `RainmakerShotMetrics`) integrated with OpenFairway simulation physics.

  - **Positive 'Detecting' Status Display**: The launch monitor connection status now displays in vibrant green when in the "Detecting" state, clearly communicating that your device is actively detecting the ball and ready to play rather than appearing as a red error condition.
  - **Unified No-Scroll Tab Bar**: Optimized tab bar sizing, margins, and concise tab labels ("📶 Bluetooth", "🌐 Other (GSPro)", "🔐 Encrypted BT", and "⛳ Bushnell (FSX)") so all connection options fit smoothly across the modal on all screen sizes with no left/right scroll arrows. Tab cycling keyboard and gamepad bumper hints ([Q / E] and [LB / RB]) are now neatly integrated into the header subtitle.
  - **High-Contrast Remember Device Checkbox**: Redesigned the "Remember device and auto-connect on startup" checkbox with custom high-contrast styling—featuring a luminous slate-blue outline when unchecked and a vivid green checkmark when enabled—ensuring the box is immediately visible and never blends into dark backgrounds.
- **Backend & Web Security Upgrades and Node 22 Transition**: Upgraded Cloud Functions, web services, and deployment pipelines to current LTS versions to enhance security and eliminate startup and container image build deprecation notices:
  - Transitioned Cloud Functions runtime stack and continuous deployment workflows to Node.js 22 LTS, resolving Node 20 end-of-life deprecation notices during container image builds.
  - Upgraded backend dependencies (`firebase-admin` to v14.5.0 and `firebase-functions` to v7.4.0), remediating legacy package vulnerabilities (including buffer bounds vulnerabilities in legacy `uuid` and dependent libraries).
  - Modernized the web client and emulator testing suites to Firebase JavaScript SDK v11.10.0.
  - Eliminated emulator and function startup warnings by explicitly setting single-project mode and pre-configuring runtime database connection endpoints.
- **Re-calibrated Green Speeds & Natural Rollout**: Re-tuned green speeds across all game modes, course play, and putting practice to address feedback that greens were rolling far too fast. The default green speed (setting 10.0) now delivers the comfortable, slower rollout previously found at speed 5.0, while setting 15.0 provides the fast pace previously found at standard 10.0. Green speed sliders now smoothly range from 5.0 (extra slow) to 16.0 (tournament fast), giving players realistic, manageable putting touch and controllable approach rollouts.

### Bug Fixes
- **Web & Local Shot Recommendations Synchronization**: Fixed an issue where swing flaw recommendations and improvement drills tracked in the simulator (e.g. Excessive Driver Spin, Pop-up launch, face-to-path curvature) appeared locally in the player profile but were not syncing or displaying online in the web portal:
  - Corrected web portal container element matching so swing recommendation and tendency cards render reliably into the DOM.
  - Added case-insensitive player lookup and fallback timestamp resolution in `cloud_sync.gd`, preventing local swing recommendations from being mistakenly purged by tombstones during merge cycles.
  - Ensured manual sync triggers ("Sync Now") always execute a full statistics pass to push newly recorded swing flaw recommendations to the cloud immediately.
- **Best Course Records Web Display**: Fixed an issue on the player portal where completed course rounds were not appearing under the "⛳ Best Course Records" section (showing "No course rounds recorded yet" despite rounds being completed). Course records now seamlessly pull from player profile records, synchronized stats documents, and match scorecards, displaying accurate score-to-par, total strokes, and 18- or 9-hole counts.
- **iOS Pre-Packaged Course Cache Availability**: Fixed an issue on iOS where the 5,000+ pre-packaged offline golf courses were not visible or selectable in the course catalog. Pre-packaged course geometries are now reliably bundled into the iOS application package, and course discovery seamlessly detects offline cached courses across both root and subfolder storage structures so all 5,000+ courses can be browsed and loaded offline without OSM rate limits or network requests.
- **Settings Menu Scrollbar Overlap**: Fixed an issue where the vertical scrollbar overlapped interactive buttons, toggle switches, dropdown selectors, and stepper controls along the right edge of the Settings menu tabs on mobile tablets and touchscreens. Each settings tab now provides generous, dynamic right margin padding so buttons and toggles remain fully unobstructed and easy to tap.
- **Remote Multiplayer Scorecard Shot Review**: Fixed an issue in online multiplayer matches where shot paths, distances, and shot review diagrams for remote opponents did not appear on the scorecard (displaying "No shots recorded for Hole"). Remote players' shots and trajectory maps are now fully recorded and rendered alongside local players on the hole review screen and scorecard.
- **Persistent Top-Left Multiplayer Queue on Hole Out**: Fixed an issue where golfers who finished a hole vanished from the top-left player order box until the start of the next hole. Players who have completed the hole now remain visible in the queue under the active golfer showing their updated score, `0 Yds` for distance, and `-` for club recommendation while remaining group members finish the hole.
- **Leaderboard Achievement Count & Web Rankings Fix**: Fixed an issue where the global leaderboard and player portal displayed 0 unlocked achievements for all players. Achievement counts now accurately synchronize between device play, player profiles, and cloud leaderboards, and the website's "Most Achievements" rankings now properly filter out zero-score entries to display active achievement rankings. In addition, the in-game personal record strip on the Leaderboards menu now displays your current unlocked achievement total.
- **Putt Detection & Longest Putt Achievements**: Fixed an issue where chip shots from on the green or fringe could mistakenly be counted as putts and unlock longest putt achievements (such as Clutch Roller, Downtown Drain, Cross-Green Sniper, or Astronomical Roll). A hit is now strictly considered as a putt only when a putter is being used, ensuring chips with wedges or irons never trigger putting camera modes, record putting distance records, or unlock putting achievements.
- **Teebox Obstruction & Hole Path Tree Clearance**: Fixed an issue where procedurally generated and satellite-scanned trees were spawning directly in front of teeboxes, in flight corridors, and on the path of play between the tee, fairway, and green. A comprehensive hole flight corridor and teebox clearance system now guarantees that generated trees will never obstruct drives in front of any teebox or encroach upon the path of the hole to the fairway and green (unless a tree was explicitly mapped at that exact location in authentic OpenStreetMap data). Backward backdrop trees behind tees are now also prevented from spawning in front of any other tee box in the tee complex.
