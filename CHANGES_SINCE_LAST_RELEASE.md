### New Features
- **In-Game Time-of-Day Slider, Night Golf & Rain Weather Experience:** Added an interactive environmental control bar seamlessly integrated into a single unified top HUD badge alongside Aim, Elevation, and Wind stats.
  - **Dynamic Time-of-Day Lighting:** Adjust the time of day and lighting angle/intensity with an expanded slider bordered by a moon on the left and sun on the right—smoothly transitioning conditions from bright afternoon sunshine to dawn, dusk, and full night golf. Deep background mountain panoramas naturally dim into dark moonlit silhouettes at night.
  - **Night Golf Visuals:** In night and dusk settings, the golf ball automatically transforms into an illuminated glow-in-the-dark ball, the flag hole lights up with a glowing luminous cup, and sleek electric blue edge lights and ground pucks outline fairway borders and greens for clear nighttime target visibility (with zero shot collision).
  - **Overcast Rain & Authentic Soft Rain Audio:** A weather toggle switches between Sun and Rain. Selecting Rain activates an immersive rain setting with dense falling raindrops (no ground splash-back), overcast cloud cover, misty atmospheric fog, diffused lighting, and authentic soft rainfall ambient sound sourced to replace daytime birdsong while rain is active.
- **Computed Launch Monitor Stats & Visual Indicators (`* Stat *`):** When launch monitors send partial delivery data, the simulator now automatically computes derived metrics if the required inputs are available. For example, Smash Factor is calculated when ball speed and clubhead speed are sent (`Ball Speed / Club Speed`), and Face to Path is calculated when face angle and club path are sent (`Face Angle - Club Path`). Whenever a metric is computed by the app rather than sent directly from the launch monitor hardware, its title is clearly distinguished with surrounding asterisks (e.g. `* Smash Factor *` and `* Face to Path *` on HUD stat tiles, swing replays, and shot summaries, versus standard titles like `Smash Factor` and `Face to Path` when provided directly by the launch monitor). Metrics not measured by the launch monitor and unable to be calculated continue to show `N/A`. Stat card titles also feature dynamic responsive font sizing to ensure text always fits cleanly without clipping.
- **Shot tracking diagrams in Match History:** The history scorecard now includes a full Shot Review section. After opening a match's scorecard from Match History, tap or click any hole number directly on the scorecard header to inspect that hole's shot paths, distances, and hole diagram for each player, matching the Course Play scorecard experience.

### Improvements
- **Full Controller Support for Weather & Time-of-Day Controls:** The lighting slider and Sun/Rain toggle switch can now be navigated and adjusted seamlessly using a game controller (D-pad, analog stick, and 'A' / `ui_accept` button). The controls are fully integrated into the top HUD controller focus graph between the Player Profile option and the top-right button row (Eye button, Home, Settings). When focused, high-visibility gold focus outlines clearly highlight each control. On the time-of-day slider, left and right controls smoothly sweep through 20 granular lighting steps from night to day, while reaching the day boundary or pressing 'A' smoothly advances focus to the weather toggle. On the weather toggle, pressing 'A' instantly toggles between Sun and Rain modes. Additionally, controls are protected from premature focus release so gamepad aiming inputs never interrupt slider adjustments.
- **Automated Cloud Cache Publishing for iOS:** Updated the monthly course sync script (`run_monthly_sync.ps1`) to automatically bundle and publish cached golf course geometries (`cached_geometry.tar`) directly to GitHub release assets (`course-cache`), guaranteeing that any new or updated courses are immediately packaged into iOS and cloud CI builds.
- **Offline Course Catalog & Pre-Packaged Course Support on iOS:** Restored full offline course geometry cache detection and packaging for iPhone and iPad builds. Players on iOS can now view and instantly generate the entire pre-packaged course catalog (5,000+ cached courses in the US and Europe) without hitting online map API limits.
- **Enhanced Bluetooth Launch Monitor Reliability on iOS:** Fixed Bluetooth GATT connection and characteristic discovery on iOS/iPadOS when connecting to launch monitors such as the Square Golf launch monitor. The peripheral delegate is now registered prior to establishing the BLE connection, redundant notification subscriptions are bypassed, and GATT characteristic mapping includes descriptive diagnostic logging to prevent connection timeouts and endless reconnect loops.
- **True Putting Green Perimeter Boundary in Night Golf:** Night golf boundary lighting around the green now extracts and traces the exact perimeter of the putting green from the course's green geometry instead of drawing a hardcoded circle centered on the hole pin. The neon blue boundary now accurately outlines the natural contours, shape, and curves of each green regardless of pin placement.
- **Continuous Neon Fairway Light & Ground-Hugging Glow:** Night golf boundary lighting now strictly follows the actual fairway perimeter (extracted from course fairway bodies) and putting green edges. Line segments are densified every 2.5 meters to precisely follow local terrain elevations, ensuring the lines lie completely flat against the turf over hills and hollows. Removed discrete point light sources and puck cylinders in favor of a continuous neon core ribbon with a wide additive radiant ground glow skirt that smoothly casts electric blue light onto the surrounding grass.
- **Dynamic Multi-Hole Night Golf Transitions:** Night fairway boundary lighting and illuminated cup glow now dynamically track hole transitions during Course Play, automatically rebuilding and updating the neon perimeter lines for Hole 2, Hole 3, and throughout the entire 18-hole round.
- **Refined Weather Pill Toggle & Icon Scaling:** The Sun/Rain toggle button now triggers when clicked anywhere on the button rather than requiring a click on a specific side. Fixed icon sizing so the Sun and Rain icons scale comfortably inside the toggle pill without overflowing or clipping.
- **Course Night Ground Illumination & Balanced Glow:** Course edge boundary lighting at night now casts vivid blue ground illumination across fairways and green edges, lighting up the adjacent turf just like real nighttime neon golf tracks. The glowing golf ball emission and local omni lighting have been toned down to a softer, balanced electric glow so it no longer over-saturates the view.
- **Night Lighting Twilight Threshold:** Adjusted the activation threshold for night golf visuals (glow ball, illuminated cup, and fairway/green edge lights) so they wait to turn on until the sky has reached true twilight and dark night conditions rather than activating prematurely at dusk.
- **Streamlined Top Aim Badge & Weather Controls:** Refined the top HUD container into a slim, low-profile badge (height reduced from 80px to 58px) with an expanded time-of-day slider that spans cleanly beneath the distance and lie info just like the updated reference design. Replaced the oversized weather toggle with a sleek, perfectly aligned capsule switch (56x22px) that seamlessly highlights the active Sun or Rain mode without overlapping icons or clutter.
- **Calibrated Fairway Rollout & Low-Launch Ball Physics:** Refined rollout and bounce physics across fairways and rough. Typical amateur driver drives now roll out a realistic 10–20 yards on fairways (averaging ~17–19 yards) instead of getting cut short, while fairway woods, hybrids, and long irons roll out with proportional, controlled forward release (12–15 yards). Low-launch, high-speed punch shots and stingers skip cleanly across fairways and release upward to 50 yards as expected. Also fixed course fairway collision detection so landing on fairways properly registers as fairway turf rather than rough. Green check, bite, and wedge backspin mechanics remain completely preserved and untouched.
- Fixed an issue on the Driving Range where 3D address grass blades generated around the player and hitting mats, and balls landing outside the center corridor suffered shot reduction penalties and rough lie resistance; all balls on the driving range now always register as on the tee box with 0% resistance, and long/3D grass blades and clumps are completely removed for a clean, manicured range.
- Fixed putting green slope grid and gimme circle placement so they lie flat against the green turf rather than floating in mid-air. Grid lines now follow the green's natural contours closely, and gimme circles properly respect 3D depth against the ball, cup, and surrounding terrain.
- Low graphics quality trees now feature a dedicated, clean stylized cartoon look with toon cel shading and smooth wood tones that harmonize with the low-poly stippled turf and stylized water.
- Disabled tall grass and shoreline reeds around water hazards on Low graphics quality to maintain a clean, lightweight visual presentation and improve performance.
- Removed bushes and low shrubbery from the driving range to ensure an unobstructed, clean outfield.
- Short shots under 20 yards (such as tap-in putts and chip-outs) no longer display a line or distance label on the shot overview diagram in the scorecard, keeping the map clean and focused on meaningful shot paths.
- The blue aim reticle circle is no longer shown when aiming on the putting green, keeping the view clean for putting. The distance card and guideline still display normally.
- **iOS File Sharing & In-Game Log Copy:** Enabled native iOS document and file sharing (`UIFileSharingEnabled`), allowing players to view and copy diagnostic log files directly from the iOS Files app (under "On My iPhone / iPad" -> "Heckle Golf Simulator") or via Mac/PC file sharing. Added a one-tap "Copy Full Log" button in Developer Settings to instantly copy all engine and diagnostic logs to the device clipboard.
- **Automatic Diagnostic Logging & Crash Log Retention on Mobile:** Diagnostic logging is now enabled by default on iOS and Android so connection and gameplay events are captured without needing to toggle settings first. Session startup now safely rotates and preserves previous logs to `debug.previous.log` rather than wiping them, ensuring crash diagnostics can always be retrieved from the Files app.
- **Smooth, Instant Club Switching During Aiming:** Eliminated the heavy frame drops and lag spikes experienced when switching clubs while aiming or at address. Fairway collision scanning now uses pre-baked 2D bounding boxes and cached aim targets, player club statistics are cached in memory instead of repeatedly reading and parsing JSON from disk, address camera line-of-sight raycasts are bypassed during active aiming, and tree occlusion evaluations are deferred during rapid club cycling.
- **Improved Course Download Reliability & Server Mirrors:** Updated OpenStreetMap course downloading to utilize high-speed, modern global Overpass API mirrors (including OpenStreetMap France, Kumi Systems, and Private.coffee) while removing deprecated subdomains that previously caused downloads to hang or time out.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue where internal developer test courses (such as Airways and DragonFly) appeared in the course selection and custom course menus on iOS and other devices. Course lists now strictly display only verified user and downloaded courses.
- Fixed an issue where cached course geometries failed to be detected within iOS application bundles due to virtual directory iteration limitations, preventing players from downloading cached courses.
- Fixed an issue where Bluetooth launch monitor connections on iOS/iPadOS could get stuck indefinitely showing "Connecting" when reconnecting or discovering device GATT characteristics.
- Fixed a crash (heap corruption) when loading courses in Course Play mode caused by premature collision raycasts and uninitialized graphics mesh descriptors during course scene setup.
- Fixed an issue on the Match History scorecard where selecting an unplayed hole displayed a shot review diagram with misleading shot paths from other holes; unplayed holes and holes without recorded shots now properly show a clear "Hole has not been played" placeholder instead of rendering a shot view.
- Fixed an issue on the Match History scorecard view where game controllers could not move between holes to inspect different hole overview shot histories. Hole header buttons are now fully focusable with gamepad directional controls (D-pad and analog sticks), include high-visibility gold focus indicator outlines, and support quick hole switching with controller Left and Right Bumpers (LB / RB). Navigating focus between hole buttons automatically updates the hole shot path diagrams and player score summaries in real-time, the scorecard scroll area automatically follows focus to keep the active hole button visible, and pressing the controller 'B' button or 'Close' cleanly closes the overlay and restores menu focus.
- Fixed an issue where the green slope heatmap on the minimap and aerial views displayed gaps, patchy holes, truncated sections on multi-body greens, or missing triangular cuts along green edges due to surrounding terrain occlusion. The heatmap now aggregates all contiguous green geometry across multi-part greens, adapts vertex elevations to prevent collar clipping, disables depth occlusion from higher surrounding turf, and isolates camera layers so slope directional arrows and putting grids never bleed through, delivering a completely seamless, solid, smooth elevation heatmap across the entire green.
- Fixed an issue where scorecard hole number buttons could not be selected with a game controller to view different hole shot path diagrams. Hole buttons are now fully focusable with controller D-pad and analog stick navigation, include a distinct gold focus highlight outline, support quick hole cycling via controller Left and Right Bumpers (LB / RB), and support tab cycling via Triggers (LT / RT).
- Fixed an issue where the Match History scorecard displayed "no data found" or "no shots recorded" when attempting to view saved shot tracking diagrams; deserialization now properly restores saved shot coordinate arrays into full Vector3 positions for diagram rendering.
- Fixed an issue where the ball could be teed up on subsequent shots if it came to rest inside a tee box area; teeing up the ball (with elevated tee peg) is now strictly restricted to the player's first shot on a hole (0 strokes taken), and balls landing on any tee box thereafter are played from the turf.
- Fixed an issue where switching clubs on the driving range did not clear the current active shot tracers from the field.
- Prevented bushes from spawning in front of tee boxes and in the flight corridor towards fairways, and ensured course bushes cluster naturally around trees.
- Fixed a crash when opening the Custom Course Creator caused by a duplicate `_exit_tree` method declaration.
- Fixed a GDScript parser type inference error on `rel_vec` during custom course generation.
- Fixed an issue where trees and bushes were occasionally placed directly on or too close to cart paths during course generation.
- Fixed an issue where random pin placement was ignored and placed the pin in the center of the green on every hole.
- Fixed an issue where gimme circles on the minimap extended past the putting surface into the fringe and rough; gimme circles across all camera views (3D, aerial, and minimap) now strictly conform to the green boundary.
- Fixed an issue where balls coming to rest off the green (such as on the collar, fringe, bunker, or rough) were incorrectly awarded gimmes; gimmes are now strictly confined to balls that come to rest on the green.
- Fixed an issue where gimmes were calculated against the default center of the green rather than the actual cup location when random pin placement was enabled; gimme awards, distance measurements, and visual rings now stay perfectly synchronized with the active pin.
- Fixed an issue where the club selection button rendered on top of modal popups (such as the shot analysis and swing replay modal, previous shot details, and confirmation dialogs). Modals now consistently display in front of all HUD elements.
- Fixed an issue where downloading a course could pull in holes from neighboring golf courses located nearby (such as Little Haven pulling in Hole 15 from adjacent Hilly Haven); course boundary matching now strictly differentiates adjacent facilities, ensuring courses only import their own holes and golf features.
- Fixed an issue where textured elevated deep rough grass did not always appear when hitting from heavy rough with 25%+ ball lie resistance; deep rough textures and 3D blade fields now consistently render whenever a lie has 25% or more resistance across all game modes, course types, and tree-lined areas.
