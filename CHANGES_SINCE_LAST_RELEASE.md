### New Features
- **Tournament Mode**: A full asynchronous tournament system is now available under the new **Tournaments** section in the Online Multiplayer menu. Tournaments let players compete at their own pace — play a course locally whenever you like, and your scores are tracked and compared against everyone else in the tournament.
  - **Host a Tournament**: Set a course, game conditions (wind, green speed, length), max player count, end date, and visibility (public or private). Share the 6-character join code with friends.
  - **Join a Tournament**: Enter a 6-character code to join any tournament before its end date and player cap.
  - **Public Tournaments Browser**: Browse all open public tournaments with filters for time period and course. See how many players have joined and jump in directly.
  - **Standings**: View ranked scorecard standings for any tournament — public or private ones you're in. Filter by participation (all tournaments vs. ones you joined), time frame, and course. Scores are ranked lowest-to-highest (stroke play). See each player's per-hole progress, to-par score, and whether their round is pending, in progress, or finalized.
  - **Batch Play Support**: No need to complete your tournament round in one sitting. Play 6 holes today, pick up where you left off tomorrow. Your progress is tracked hole by hole and synced to the tournament automatically.
  - **Automatic Score Sync**: Tournament scores are pushed to the cloud on every game-over, each time you open the app (on login), and every 30 minutes during active play — so your standings are always up to date even mid-round.
  - **Winner Locking**: Once the tournament end date passes, scores are locked and standings are finalized automatically the next time anyone views the tournament.
- **Matches / Tournaments Mode Selector**: The Online Multiplayer menu now opens with a top-level **Matches** / **Tournaments** toggle, keeping regular live multiplayer matches and asynchronous tournaments clearly separated.
- **Online Lobby Course Download Status Visibility**: In online multiplayer lobbies, players can now see real-time status when an opponent or fellow golfer is actively downloading a required course (`"Downloading Course..."` in warm amber) instead of only seeing `"Missing Course"`. This makes it immediately clear who is actively preparing their course geometry so everyone knows exactly what the lobby is waiting on.
- **Player Portal Community Link**: Added a quick-access Player Portal web community button to the top-right header of the main menu alongside the Discord and YouTube buttons, allowing golfers to open the web portal with a single tap or click.


### Improvements
- **Putting Camera High-Framerate 30 FPS Hardware Streaming**: Putting camera performance on desktop webcams has been significantly improved from a sluggish ~4 FPS to a smooth 30 FPS. The putting camera now streams hardware-compressed MJPEG video directly into the game at standard definition (640x480 @ 30 FPS), eliminating USB bandwidth bottlenecks and providing responsive ball rolls and precise putt detection.
- **Dedicated Putting Camera Pipeline**: Local putting cameras now run on an independent, dedicated lightweight camera pipeline completely separate from the golfer body tracking system. This avoids device conflicts, reduces CPU overhead, and ensures both cameras can operate smoothly and simultaneously without interfering with each other.
- **Per-Graphic Download Settings Synchronization**: When downloading a course, each graphics preset (Low, Medium, and High) now maintains its own independent settings. Customizing options such as highways, buildings, cart paths, or trees for Medium or Low settings is remembered and embedded directly into the course, allowing downloaded courses to play exactly as configured for each graphics level.

### Bug Fixes
- **Windows Desktop C# Compilation Fix**: Fixed a compilation error (`CS7036`) in the launch monitor Bluetooth Apple interop layer during Windows desktop release builds caused by a missing overload signature for Objective-C boolean message sending.
- **iPad & iOS Launch Monitor Bluetooth Connection (Square Golf)**: Fixed an issue where the Connect Launch Monitor modal on iPad would endlessly display "Scanning..." and fail to discover Square Golf launch monitors. CoreBluetooth scanning options now properly register allow-duplicates flags via native runtime symbols so peripheral advertisements and scan responses are captured, Square Golf service UUIDs (`86601001` / `86601000`) and manufacturer payloads are recognized during device discovery, and device filtering recognizes all Square Golf naming conventions (`SquareGolf(XXXX)`, `SQ`, `EXGOLF`, and `OMNI`).
- **iPad & iOS Course Download & Generation Fix**: Fixed a critical bug on iOS where downloading or generating courses from OpenStreetMap (such as Arcadia Bluffs Golf Club) would falsely state "Successfully generated course!" while failing to save `course.json` due to ahead-of-time reflection restrictions on Apple devices, leaving downloaded courses missing from the course selection list. Course metadata serialization now uses native, reflection-free formatting, ensuring downloaded courses save reliably and appear immediately in the course list.
- **iPad & iOS Driving Range Terrain Rendering**: Fixed an issue where loading the driving range on iPad displayed a blank blueish-gray screen with missing ground and fairway terrain. Metal shader pipeline compilation errors on Apple GPUs have been resolved by eliminating invalid camera matrix operations in directional shadow passes, replacing dynamic matrix inversion in slope indicators with transpose transformations, removing mipmap sampling requests on screen textures, and ensuring driving range turf shaders are optimized for mobile graphics hardware.
- **Remote Multiplayer Missing Course & Repeated Download Loop**: Fixed an issue where remote players were repeatedly flagged with 'Missing Course' and prompted to download the course before every match, even when the course was already installed or when playing back-to-back matches on the same course. Verification now checks installed course presence and GPS proximity rather than rejecting identical courses due to minor cross-platform geometry variations, pre-validates courses on join to prevent presence race conditions, and preserves cached geometry tags so downloads pull fast local data.
- **Medium Graphics Highways & Buildings Display**: Fixed an issue where courses played on Medium graphics settings did not display highways, roads, clubhouses, or surrounding buildings even when "highways & buildings" was checked in download settings. The course playback engine now respects whatever options are enabled for each graphics setting, correctly showing surrounding features and enabling their physical collision on Medium (and any other setting where checked).
- **Putting Camera Setup Window Auto-Close & Header Close Button**: Fixed an issue where closing or minimizing the putting camera modal left the Camera Setup window stuck open on screen with no way to close it. The setup window now automatically closes when the camera modal is closed or minimized, features a prominent "✕" close button in its header bar, auto-scales to fit all display sizes so the bottom close button remains visible, and supports closing via the Escape key.
- **Google Play Deployment Guard for Reused Artifacts**: Prevented duplicate version code errors when deploying releases with reused Android packages. Google Play deployment is now automatically skipped for previously built AAB packages while remaining active for newly built artifacts.
- **Windows & macOS Build Pipeline Reliability**: Restored synchronous process streaming and graceful package finalization during Godot headless exports, ensuring non-fatal teardown warnings do not trigger spurious build failures.
- **Fixed Plant Asset Scene Encoding in Windows Builds**: Resolved a parser error (`Parse Error: Expected '['`) caused by hidden UTF-8 Byte Order Marks (BOM) in exterior plant static body scenes.
- **Online Multiplayer Realtime Event Transmission & Turn Handshake**: Fixed a critical bug where online shot events and turn updates were never published to the cloud backend after hitting the ball due to an uninitialized database client reference, leaving remote players perpetually waiting on the opponent's first shot. Shot events and turn transitions are now published reliably with active cloud verification.
- **Fixed Release Build Freezes on Windows**: Resolved an issue where headless Godot exports would hang on Windows after finalizing the game package (`[DONE] savepack`) due to Mono domain teardown deadlock. Added automatic package completion detection and process lifecycle management to guarantee smooth automated builds.
- **Online Multiplayer Shot Replay & Turn Progression**: Fixed an issue where matches would get stuck on "Waiting for player to hit..." after the first shot. Opponent shots now cleanly replay with ball tracer trails and follow-camera tracking on remote screens before handing over the next turn.
- **Launch Monitor Readiness HUD in Multiplayer**: The "Ball Ready" status indicator and ready placement box are now hidden when waiting for your opponent to hit or when viewing a remote shot replay, reappearing only when it is your turn to hit.
- **Menu Screen Readiness Indicator**: Fixed an issue where the launch monitor "Ball Ready" indicator and square ready zone incorrectly displayed inside multiplayer lobby setup screens, leaderboards, and menu pages.
- **macOS Graphics Startup Freeze & AMD Shader Compatibility**: Fixed an issue where the game would freeze on launch ~95% of the time on macOS systems (particularly Intel Macs with AMD Radeon GPUs). Clustered compute shader compilation errors in MoltenVK have been resolved by configuring macOS builds to use the robust Mobile Vulkan backend, ensuring quick, smooth, freeze-free startups across Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
- **Downloaded Course Load Failure & Gray Screen Trap**: Fixed an issue where opening a course with a corrupted file or parse error resulted in an unrecoverable blank gray screen with no camera, UI, or way to exit. Course loading now features robust error detection with automatic fallback between `.scn` and `.tscn` formats. If a course geometry file is unreadable, the game immediately presents a clean error dialog with options to return to Course Selection or the Main Menu, along with full keyboard (Escape) and controller navigation. Furthermore, corrupted courses are highlighted directly in the course selection list (`[Corrupted - Redownload]`) with informative alerts so players can delete and redownload them cleanly without getting stuck in a loading screen.
- **Driver Ball Speed Lie Reduction on Tee Shots**: Fixed a bug where tee shots on hole 1 could be erroneously penalized with a rough lie penalty (reducing driver ball speed by ~14%, e.g. 145 mph down to ~125 mph) when courses had non-standard tee collision geometry. The simulation engine now guarantees that the first shot of any hole is always treated as a tee box shot with zero lie penalty, and raw launch monitor telemetry is preserved without modification.
- **Floating Tee Markers and Divot Sand Caddies**: Fixed an issue where spherical tee markers (red, white, blue, etc.) and green divot sand boxes could occasionally spawn floating in the air above the teebox. Terrain height queries now filter out obstacle colliders and dynamic props, previously generated teebox containers are cleanly detached before rebuilding so raycasts do not collide with previous markers, and marker vertical offsets are clamped against localized height anomalies to ensure they always rest firmly on the ground.
- **Unstickered Club Delivery Data Filtering**: Fixed an issue where clubs without silver shaft tracking stickers displayed phantom club path, face angle, and attack angle metrics on launch monitor tiles. Club delivery metrics are now strictly validated against hardware measurement validity bitmasks and are only displayed when shaft tracking stickers are physically detected by the launch monitor cameras.
- **Multiplayer Turn Desync, Aim Flipping & Repeat Hit Issues**: Fixed an issue in multiplayer matches where if a player was further away from the hole and hit again, their subsequent shots would fly in random directions (or 180° backwards towards the tee) on the other player's screen and their ball position would appear stuck hundreds of yards away at the tee box. Default aim calculations now automatically recalibrate from the golfer's actual ball position down the fairway towards the green for every shot, preventing stale aim directions and backwards flips. Ball spawn points and starting positions are also kept synchronized across shots so both players see the true location and trajectory of every ball.
- **Multiplayer Gimme & Hole Completion Desync**: Fixed a bug where a player who reached gimme range remained active with the game still saying it was their turn, while the remote player's screen showed them 200+ yards away. Gimmes and holed-out states are now immediately synchronized across both screens, and turn updates arriving during shot replays are queued and applied cleanly once the flight finishes, properly advancing the turn to the remaining golfer or completing the hole.
- **Smooth Multiplayer Ball Flight Camera Tracking**: Fixed an issue where the main camera during an opponent's shot would sometimes stay stationary and jump directly to the landing spot at the end of the shot instead of smoothly following the ball through the air. The follow camera now tracks the remote ball smoothly throughout its full trajectory arc from launch to rest.
